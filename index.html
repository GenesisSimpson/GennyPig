Meet the pig: bright, social, and full of surprises

Pigs are domestic mammals with a long history alongside people. They use their keen senses to explore the world around them.

🧠

Quick learners

Pigs can learn routines and solve simple tasks. Their curiosity makes exploring and enrichment part of a happy day.

🐷

Social by nature

Pigs communicate with sounds and body language, and they often enjoy the company of other pigs.

🌱

Born explorers

A pig's snout is an excellent tool for investigating scents and rooting around in the ground for food.

Piggy trivia

Four little facts to share

There's always something new to learn when you look at the world from a pig's point of view.

Snout sensePigs have a strong sense of smell, which helps them explore and find food.
Chatty friendsPigs make a range of sounds, including grunts and squeals.
Mud mattersWallowing can help pigs cool down; pigs have limited ability to sweat.
Many breedsDomestic pigs come in many sizes, colors, and coat patterns.

Good care means room to be a pig.

Pigs need thoughtful, lifelong care. Their wellbeing depends on a safe environment that lets them move, explore, rest, and socialize.

  • Provide shelter, clean water, and suitable food.
  • Make space for movement and natural rooting behavior.
  • Offer companionship and regular veterinary care.
